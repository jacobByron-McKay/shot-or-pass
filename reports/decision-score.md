# The decision score

How often a player shoots when a better pass was on, less how often a typical
player shooting from the same spots would. Below zero is the better decider.
It is read within position, because where a player stands explains a lot of
it.

## On every women's shot in the open data

32,999 shots with a freeze frame, from 13 competition-seasons and 1,822 players.
A pass was the better option on 58.9% of them. Distance, angle and headers
explain 12.2% of that; the decision score is what is left.

Across 227 players with 40 or more shots, the two halves of a player's shots
agree at 0.69, and a player needs about 38 shots before the score can be
trusted. One season predicts the next at 0.71, over 51 pairs of seasons.

Read within position, the halves agree at 0.43, which takes about 109 shots.
That is the honest figure for comparing two forwards.

## The men's replication

The same score on 18,960 men's shots: the Premier League and La Liga in 2015/16,
and Leverkusen's matches in 2023/24. One season per league, so split-half
only. Across 139 players with 40 or more shots the halves agree at 0.60, and it
takes about 50 shots to trust a player's score. Weaker than the women's, on a
smaller set, and kept separate from it.

Data: StatsBomb open data.
